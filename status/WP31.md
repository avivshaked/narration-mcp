## Done
- **`narration.jobs`**, the engine as a library with a thin adapter behind the daemon's `JobRunner`:
  - `host`: WP30's seam, mirrored member for member. It includes the reviewed changes: `has_work`,
    `ResidencyError`, and the note that a worker may be stopped between jobs.
  - `engine.JobEngine`: plans a `generate` or `analyse` job from its request and the cache, then does one
    piece of work per step (one render, one post-processing or one scoring, or one bounded wait).
    - **Rounds** (§4 item 4, §8): every render of a round on Qwen, then post-processing on the CPU, then
      scoring on the QA group. Each group loads once a round.
    - **Retakes:** a take slot whose take has a retake-trigger flag (`codes.is_retake_trigger`, including
      DC-12) gets the next attempt number above every attempt of its segment, in slot order, up to
      `max_retakes`.
    - **The cache answers first** (§4 item 6, §10.2). Keys come from the request and the pins only. Each
      layer is looked up at planning and again at claim time, and made under a lease. Work in flight
      under another holder is waited for (`Store.wait_for`), never made twice. A resubmission walks the
      same attempts, retakes included, from the cache.
    - **Execution problems** are flagged on their segment with severity `error`, and the job goes on:
      - out-of-memory: unload, wait, retry once, then `GPU_OOM`;
      - a crash or timeout: retry once in a new worker, then `WORKER_CRASHED`;
      - lost models or a lost prepared voice: load again, once;
      - otherwise `RENDER_FAILED` or `QA_UNAVAILABLE`.
    - **Job-level failures** fail the job with the error:
      - `BACKEND_NOT_INSTALLED`: no pinned engine, a malformed profile, or a load the worker refuses;
      - `ENGINE_CHANGED`, `VOICE_NOT_SYNTHETIC` (§17.4, also checked here), `VOICE_NOT_MEASURED`,
        `VOICE_FILE_MISMATCH`, `GPU_UNAVAILABLE`, `STORE_FULL`.
    - **Endings:** after scoring, each segment's suggestion and the per-job consistency report
      (`SPK_OUTLIER`). Neither changes a verdict.
    - A job cancelled mid-way keeps what it finished; its unfinished segments are `skipped` with
      `CANCELLED`.
    - **Job record:** after every step, the job's `items` (attempts with `fresh`, `RETAKEN`,
      `CANARY_MISMATCH` and `SPK_OUTLIER`), `round`, `phase`, `message` and `progress` are written.
      `progress` is in estimated audio seconds: its total grows with retakes, and it never goes back.
    - **Cached analyses** hold nothing request-specific: `received` is the spoken text, and flags carry
      no segment id.
  - `runner.EngineRunner`: the claim loop.
    - It claims interactive jobs first, then first come first served.
    - It re-reads the job every step: a cancel is finished, and a batch job gives way between pieces to a
      queued interactive job, then resumes from the cache.
    - An unexpected exception fails the job as `INTERNAL` instead of looping.
    - `has_work` claims nothing. `shutdown` gives the job back with `return_job`.
    - It refreshes the drain estimate after each step.
    - `default_runner()` is the zero-argument factory for the daemon's `--runner`, and `build_runner()`
      is the explicit build.
  - `gpu.Residency`: one resident group, reused across jobs and loaded again after a worker restart.
    - The free-VRAM check is need + `[gpu] min_free_margin_mb`, through an injectable probe (`NvmlProbe`:
      memory totals only, never processes).
    - While it waits, the phase is `waiting_for_gpu`, the check repeats every 15 s through `host.sleep`,
      and `waiting_since` is published.
    - After `wait_timeout_min` the job fails with `GPU_UNAVAILABLE`, with `retry_after_s` and the facts
      behind it.
  - `admission`: DC-2's numbers as pure functions, shared with WP36:
    - `poll_after_s`;
    - `retry_after_s` for `QUEUE_FULL` and `RATE_LIMITED` (the rate window, by bisection over
      `jobs_created_since`), and for `GPU_UNAVAILABLE`;
    - `est_drain_s`, `eta_s`, `queue_position` and the `admission` facts;
    - a measured `Throughput`.
  - `plan`, `pins`, `voice`, `hooks`:
    - `plan` reads a request with `[defaults]`.
    - `pins` builds Qwen's `load` with every audio-changing setting explicit (§10.1), and each call's
      `max_new_tokens` from the profile (DC-4). Missing settings are refused, never defaulted.
    - `voice` keeps the clip's working copy in `scratch/voices/<sha256>.wav`.
    - `hooks.EngineGuard` is where WP32's canary gate plugs in (`NoGuard` until then).
- **Two bugs found by the new tests and fixed, each with a regression test that fails on the old code:**
  - the in-flight wait picked work queued behind the in-flight piece, returned at once, and spun;
  - a cancel landing as the job finished left it `cancelling` for good.

## Decisions made (and why)
- **One piece per step.** The daemon's stop, cancel and preemption act between pieces, which are all
  shorter than a segment's work (the seam's contract).
- **QA waits for the whole round's renders**, so each group loads once a round (§4 item 4). The cost is
  that a round's first scores come only after its last render.
- **`max_retakes` counts per take slot.** With `takes: 2`, each slot may be retaken twice. Retake attempt
  numbers are shared by the segment's slots, so they never collide (see Questions).
- **Execution errors are not retaken.** `GPU_OOM`, `WORKER_CRASHED`, `RENDER_FAILED` and `QA_UNAVAILABLE`
  are §14's segment-level problems, not QA verdicts, and the retake rule reads QA flags only.
- **A load the worker refuses fails the job.** The pinned models cannot run, so the job fails with
  `BACKEND_NOT_INSTALLED` or `INTERNAL`, not take by take. Out-of-memory at load is the exception, and is
  retried.
- **What a worker holds is keyed to its process.** Model residency and the prepared voice are recorded
  by pid, and reset on every load. So a worker the daemon stopped or unloaded between jobs is loaded and
  prepared again (WP30's review, item 2).
- **Job selection is strict priority, then first come.** "Affinity" (§4) is served by keeping the
  resident group loaded across jobs. The queue is never reordered for it, so a batch job is never
  starved by later ones that happen to match the resident model.
- **`analyse` runs the same pipeline as `generate`.** The cache decides what is made, so a scoring-only
  job loads only the QA group.
- **Kinds this engine does not run** (`design`, `measure`, `profile`, `pronunciation`) fail at claim
  with `INTERNAL`, a hint, and not retryable. Left queued, they would never run (see Questions).
- **`default_runner` fails every job with `BACKEND_NOT_INSTALLED`** until the cue aligner (WP15) and the
  QA models' pins (WP22) can be assembled (`installed_engine`). It never renders takes it cannot check.
- **Suggestions and the consistency report are kept in `JobRecord.result`**, as
  `{suggestions: [{segment_id, take_id, suggestion}], consistency}`, so WP36 need not recompute them
  (see Contract change requests).
- **The clip's working copy lives in `scratch/voices/<sha256>.wav`.** When the copy is missing, the
  engine copies the clip again from the request's path, but only if the file still has the sha256 that
  was sent. Otherwise it fails with `VOICE_FILE_MISMATCH`. The §17.3 path check is injected
  (`check_path`).
- **Starting constants, all ASSUME:**
  - `OOM_WAIT_S` = 15 s;
  - `DEFER_S` = 2 s;
  - lease TTLs of 1 h for renders and scoring, 10 min for post-processing;
  - DC-2's `WALL_PER_AUDIO_S` = 3.0, `MODEL_LOAD_S` = 20 and `CHARS_PER_AUDIO_S` = 15, which the
    engine's `Throughput` replaces with measurements.

## What WP30's seam lacks (for WP30 or the lead)
- **`hello` on the pool's clients**: see the contract change request above.
- **The platform in `RunnerHost`.** Without it, the zero-argument `default_runner` cannot wire the §17.3
  path check for the caller's clip.
  - It matters only when the working copy is missing at run time: the front-end copies the clip at
    submit.
  - Either add `platform` to `RunnerHost`, or let the runner factory take the platform.
