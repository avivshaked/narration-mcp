## Decisions made (and why)
- **The lead's decisions on the follow-up report (2026-09-26).** All six are built (see Done).
  1. **Affinity: accepted as built.** Jobs are claimed by priority, then first come. Affinity means the
     resident group is kept across a job boundary; it never reorders the queue.
  2. **One job at a time: the lead's decision, pending the owner.** Cross-job grouping within a round is
     not in this merge; WP31 merges as the one-job-at-a-time engine. The lead is putting the grouping to
     the owner as a scope question; if the owner wants it, it becomes a follow-up package on top of this
     one. Its gain is corrected: a Qwen load measured 3.7 s on the H1 run (warm file cache), so a swap
     pair is likely nearer 10 to 15 s than 40 s (BELIEVE; the QA load is not measured yet).
  3. **`embed`'s device: no protocol change.** `"cuda"` means "the GPU the QA group was loaded on" (the
     load's `[gpu] device`). The lead tells WP22 to embed on the loaded group's device.
  4. **The two requests to WP30 were mine to do after WP30 merged**, authorised in `narration.daemon` and
     `narration.platform`, each with a test: `CUDA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID`, and the log's name in one
     place.
  5. **Open items closed:** WP32 assembles `installed_engine`; the lead tells WP22 about `"English"` as
     the ASR language; WP36's result-assembly duties are recorded in HANDOFF.
  6. **Contracts renumber:** to 1.6.5, after WP16's follow-ups (1.6.3) and WP30 (1.6.4).
- **One keeper thread per engine, for the engine's life** (fix A).
  - A thread that ended when idle would leave its connection behind at each restart, so the thread
    waits idle instead, and `close` at shutdown stops it.
  - When a block ends, it waits (up to 30 s, `END_WAIT_S`) for any renewal of its lease that is already
    under way, so a lease is never renewed after its work ends.
- **`CANARY_MISMATCH` needs the tier to be `bit_exact`.** A profile whose tier is not yet measured (None)
  gets no flag either, since §14 says "`bit_exact` tier only".
- **`CUDA_DEVICE_ORDER` replaces an operator's value.** NVML's numbering cannot be changed, so CUDA's must
  follow it for `[gpu] device` to mean one GPU.
- **The log's name lives in `narration.daemon.settings`**, the daemon's standard-library-only module. So
  the entry point can import it before the thread cap, and the engine can import it without importing
  `__main__`.
- **The segment is the unit of the OOM retry** (§4 item 5: "then fail the segment"), for a load and a
  render alike. After the retry, only the take slots waiting for the same stage are failed.
- **A prepare failure is always `UNSUPPORTED_AUDIO`**, unless it is OOM, a missing backend or lost
  models. `prepare_voice` only reads the clip and encodes it, and every take needs it. The worker's code
  is kept in `details.worker_code`.
- **Affinity does not reorder the queue** (accepted as decision 1). First come is a strict order (the
  store's insertion sequence), so affinity ranked after it could never decide anything.
- **Lease TTL of 300 s, with renewal every 60 s.** A lost lease is not renewed again, and the store
  publishes the first result.
- **`MAX_CLIP_BYTES` is 20 MiB** ("20 MB" in §17.3). WP36's submit check should use the same constant.
- **`stage_clip` refuses to read a caller's path without a check** (`INTERNAL`, not `PATH_NOT_ALLOWED`),
  because that is a build fault, not the caller's. The engine now always has a check: its own, or the
  daemon's platform.
- **A write into the store that fails for a reason other than a full disk** is raised as it is, and the
  runner records it as `INTERNAL`.
- **The job record's round never goes back.** A job taken again whose finished rounds are all in the
  cache resumes its round exactly. If a round had an execution error, which is never cached, the error
  is made again, and the record keeps showing the higher round.
- Earlier decisions stand:
  - one piece of work per step;
  - QA waits for the round's renders;
  - `max_retakes` is per slot, and execution errors are not retaken (both confirmed);
  - `analyse` runs the same pipeline;
  - `default_runner` fails with `BACKEND_NOT_INSTALLED` until `installed_engine` is assembled (WP32).

## Questions for the lead / owner
1. **Cross-job grouping within a round (§4 item 3)** is with the owner as a scope question (decision 2).
   The estimate stands:
   - about 300 to 400 lines in `narration.jobs` and 300 lines of tests;
   - plus a change to the seam and the status contract (WP30, §7.6);
   - roughly two to three days.

   The gain is one Qwen↔QA swap pair per extra job in a round. That is BELIEVE, about 10 to 15 s: a Qwen
   load measured 3.7 s on the H1 run with a warm file cache, and the QA load is not measured.
2. **`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`** (new; BELIEVE, not tested here). CUDA numbers only the visible GPUs, while
   NVML numbers them all. So an operator who sets this variable in the daemon's environment could again
   make `[gpu] device` mean two different GPUs. Refusing it or warning about it belongs in `doctor` or the
   supervisor; I changed nothing.
