## Decisions made (and why)
- **The meter counts whole blocks only.** pyloudnorm rounds its block count, so it also counted a final
  block that runs past the end, as if silence followed.
  - KNOW: on the bakeoff paragraphs that read up to 0.13 LU low under the 5.4 trim, beyond EBU Tech
    3341's 0.1 LU.
  - `integrated_loudness` gives pyloudnorm the signal up to the end of its last whole block. The samples
    after it lie in no whole block, so the standard measures nothing there.
  - The meter's tool string says "whole blocks only". Under 400 ms, the take is still measured as one
    block with silence after it.
- **The meter's filter class is `"DeMan"`**: BS.1770-4's own coefficients at 48 kHz. The reviewer
  confirmed them to 1e-13.
- **The resampler filter** replaces scipy's default: flat to 11 kHz and ≤ −75 dB from 13 kHz (KNOW:
  `freqz`).
- **The true-peak ceiling** is checked on the final quantised file. When it is exceeded, the gain drops at
  once to what the ceiling allows, then in 0.0001 dB steps.
- **The gain is rounded to 0.0001 dB before it is applied**, so the record's `gain_db` is the gain used.
  This makes a cross-machine byte difference rare, not impossible. The promise is §10.1's: one machine,
  the same pins.
- **Fades stay raised cosine** (the optional change to linear was not taken). Many real takes start or end
  with little silence (DC-9), and a raised cosine is the smoother onset there.
- **`GAIN_HIGH` compares the gain applied** (the record's `gain_db`) with +12 dB.
- **Degenerate takes are delivered, not refused**, so that QA fails them:
  - non-finite samples are treated as 0;
  - silence is not trimmed and gets no gain, and its loudness is null;
  - an empty raw gives an empty file.
- **`SignalStats`**:
  - clipping counts raw samples with |x| ≥ 32767/32768, infinities included;
  - the DC offset is the raw mean;
  - the voiced bounds and the longest silence use DC-10's rule on the delivery, at its defaults
    (−40 dB, −70 dBFS);
  - the delivery's fade length at each end is left unmeasured (F1). Within it, a DC offset the delivery
    keeps is only a ramp.
    - `speech_frames(…, edge=n)` measures each frame over its samples outside the edges and takes the
      mean over those samples. A frame with none left is silence and stays out of the percentile.
    - The trim uses `edge=0`, which is unchanged.
    - The fade length comes from `DeliveryPipeline(fade_s=…)`, because a delivery file does not say
      how long its fades are. It defaults to `DeliveryConfig().fade_s`.
    - This changes a `SignalStats` rule. That makes no difference before a release, but by the contract's
      note a future change of this kind needs a new `QA_PROFILE`.
- **`POST_RULES` stays `narration.post/1`.** Nothing has been released, so everything here is version 1.
  Any later byte-affecting change bumps it, including the `GAIN_HIGH` threshold or message.
- **Evidence tests never write bakeoff-derived audio.** The by-path test was dropped; the synthetic tests
  cover `process()`.
