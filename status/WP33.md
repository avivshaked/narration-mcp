## Done
- **The seam in the job engine** (`2a22dc0`, `refactor(jobs)`, only `stages` and `state`; the lead's ruling 1):
  `Stages.measurement_key(run, seg)` and `Stages.scoring_facts(run, seg)` say what a take's analysis is keyed
  by and scored against. Their defaults are the voice's finished measurement, exactly as before
  (`tests/jobs/test_stage_hooks.py`: each default unchanged, and both refuse a run with no measurement).
  `JobRun.measurement` may be None for a kind that overrides them.
- **The `measure` job kind** (`narration.measure`, `d880583`), a `JobHandler` the runner dispatches to. It
  shares the job engine's core (residency, throughput, canary, `LeaseKeeper`) and its stages, so each take is
  rendered, post-processed and scored as a generation take is, cache first, under a lease. In order:
  1. **The transcript check** (`transcript`): Whisper on the clip, compared as QA compares a take (the number
     reader, a word alignment). `REF_TEXT_MISMATCH` (`field: voice.transcript`, `details.heard`) before
     anything is rendered. The clip's speaker embedding is taken in the same load.
  2. **The calibration set and the trend band**, in one Qwen load, then one QA load. Calibration takes are
     scored with no speaker or pace check and no measurement key. The anchor and the baseline follow
     (`baseline`), and then the band's takes are scored against them.
  3. **The trend, `tol` and each band rung judged** (`ladder`). Then the rungs above the band, one at a
     time, while every rung so far passes. The first failing rung ends the ladder, and nothing above it is
     rendered.
  4. **`measurement.json`** is published through `Store.put_measurement`. `JobRecord.result` holds its
     handle: `voice_hash`, `engine_profile`, `measurement_key`, `path`, `max_segment_chars`,
     `max_segment_seconds`, and `ladder_stopped_at` (paragraph, chars, reasons).
  - A measurement whose key is current completes the job at once.
  - A take the engine could not make fails the job, retryably: `GPU_UNAVAILABLE` for memory, `INTERNAL`
    otherwise, both with `retry_after_s` 60. The cache resumes it.
  - The outcome is `needs_attention` when no rung passed or a calibration take failed QA (design 5.12's
    spirit); otherwise `all_passed`.
- **The corpus loader** (`corpus.load_corpus`; the lead's ruling 3):
  - it checks each file against the manifest's size and sha256, and refuses a path outside the set;
  - the design text comes first in the calibration set, from `paragraphs.json`'s `design_text` (ruling 2).
    A draft may lack it; a frozen set may not;
  - the invented names become term-only hints (ruling 4);
  - `corpus_version` is `<set>@sha256:<manifest sha256>`.
- **For WP36** (`lookup`):
  - `lookup_measurement(store, voice_hash=, engine_profile_id=)`;

## Decisions made (and why)
- **Calibration analyses carry no measurement key** (`versions.measurement` None). A generation request
  can never reuse one in place of an analysis with the speaker check. Ladder analyses carry the key of the
  measurement being built, which is known before the ladder runs.
- **The band is rendered together**, because the trend that judges each band rung needs all of them. Rungs
  above the band go one at a time, so early stop saves their renders.
- **"The median `wer_adj` passes"** means the medians of `wer_adj` and word errors do not meet QA's fail
  rule (ruling 4). A rung also fails on any seed's exact-span mismatch, head insertion or token-cap hit, or
  on a median similarity below `anchor_p5 - sim_warn_margin`.
- **The transcript rule is ASSUME**: QA's fail rule, over 6 % with at least 2 word errors. It is to be
  measured on designed clips in WP40.
- **`max_segment_chars` is the top passing rung's own spoken length** (for example 151 for `ladder-150`),
  because that is what the voice read. `max_segment_seconds` is the median delivered duration there.
- **The trend is OLS over one point per band rung** (median pace by seed), and flat for a single length.
  `tol` = max(`pace_tol_min`, the band's largest seed spread).
- **The anchor** is the unit-mean of the clip and the calibration takes. `consistency_p5` is taken over the
  takes against their own centroid, and percentiles are numpy's linear ones.
- **A take-level execution error fails the whole job**, retryably. A measurement short of a take is not
  the measurement of §3.2, and the cache makes the resubmission cheap.
- **`MeasurementRecord.corpus`** is the corpus version string (`<set>@sha256:<hex>`), not the bare set id.

## Follow-ups (agreed with the review; not done now)
- (Low) A `generate` job of exactly a ladder paragraph, at a ladder attempt, under the same measurement,
  reuses that rung's analysis. That analysis has the speaker check but no pace check. It is rare and
  harmless, but if a pace check must always run, the ladder analyses need a key of their own (a contract
  change).
- (Display only) A rung that passes on its medians with one failing seed shows the segment state `warned`:
  a passing rung shows any seed worse than `pass` as `warned`. The ladder table and the measurement are
  right.
