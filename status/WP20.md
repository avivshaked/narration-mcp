## Decisions made (and why)

- **The per-call cap is checked twice.** The handler uses WP16's `require_max_new_tokens`. The engine
  checks again, and checks that the talker received exactly that cap, because the spikes call the engine
  directly.
- **The history rewrite:** `git reset --soft main` and 18 new commits, never touching main. The old commits
  exist only in this worktree's reflog. They were never pushed.
- **The clip allowlist comes from the environment**, not a file. The bakeoff's `voicelock.csv` has no
  hashes, and a gitignored file would be one more thing to keep in step.
- **The GPU contract class uses the contract's own sample `design`** ("Hello there."). It needs no material
  and no clip, and it is short (the class took 1 min in all).

## Questions for the lead / owner

- **Where the worker's reply fields still differ from the fake's.** After #19, the fake matches the real
  worker on the frame rate (12.5), `new_tokens` (cap − 1 when cut), the cap minimum (2) and the echo.
  Three differences remain in `load`:
  - the fake's `load` accepts a ceiling of 1 (`require_int(..., minimum=1)`), while qwen3 refuses a ceiling
    below 2;
  - the fake's `load` defaults a missing `max_new_tokens` to 8192, while qwen3 requires all ten sampling
    values;
  - the fake's `load` replies `vram_mb: null`, while qwen3 replies a number on CUDA.
- **Please add `NARRATION_SPIKE_ALLOW_SHA256` to `AGENTS.local.md`'s shell set-up**, with the d2 and d4
  clip hashes (from the bakeoff's clips). Without it, the spikes that clone and the acceptance test exit or
  skip with a hint. The values must stay out of tracked files.
- **Heard spellings of the invented name in spike (f).** Whisper's transcriptions of the name are
  transcripts of the bakeoff's takes, the same kind of text as section 1's one quoted example.
  `check_private` passes them. Publish none of them, and only numbers?
- **Other workers must not write WAVs with `soundfile`'s defaults** (ADR 0002, decision 5). A shared
  writer in `narration_worker` would suit.
- **A take cut by the cap is not a prefix of the uncut take** (DC-4 spike). WP14 and WP40 should not assume
  it is.
