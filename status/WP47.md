## Decisions made (and why)
- **Pauses from the signal stage's silence segmentation, not the aligner's word times.** The CTC word span
  runs from a word's first letter's first frame to its last letter's end frame (align/core.py step 1), so
  blank-labelled speech at word edges falls outside it (BELIEVE: word sums would undercount speaking time by a
  varying amount); energy frames are the same rule as the voiced span and exist for every take with one.
- `MIN_PAUSE_S` 0.25 s: BELIEVE (the usual silent-pause floor in speech-rate research, after Goldman-Eisler);
  KNOW only that 65 of 66 between-sentence gaps of the bake-off takes held silence under a similar rule
  (spikes/b-forced-align-cpu/calibration.json). ASSUME until measured on the service's own takes.
- I tried to measure pause lengths on the bake-off's clone takes (numpy only); the permission classifier
  denied it (PII handling), so no such spike exists. Measuring on the re-measured voice is the next evidence.
